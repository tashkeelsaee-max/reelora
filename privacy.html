Reelora Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Reelora ("the app") is a video and design editor. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled. The developer is Tashkeel Saee ("we", "us").

1. Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, or advertising ID. We do not show third-party ads and we do not sell your data.

2. Your media and projects

Photos, videos, audio and projects you add to Reelora are processed on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers. Project settings are stored locally on your device and are removed if you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

3. Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see or store your card or bank details. To unlock Pro features, the app sends your Google Play purchase token and product ID to our server, which checks it with Google Play to confirm that your subscription is active. This check returns only whether the subscription is active and when it expires. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in Google Play: Play Store > Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.

4. Server logs

Our verification server is hosted on Cloudflare. Like most web services, Cloudflare may process technical data such as IP address and request time to deliver the service and prevent abuse. We do not use this data to identify you.

5. Children

Reelora is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data deletion

Because we do not keep an account or personal profile for you, there is nothing for us to delete beyond what is stored on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes all local data. For any question about your data, contact us at the email below.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the date above updated.

8. Contact
